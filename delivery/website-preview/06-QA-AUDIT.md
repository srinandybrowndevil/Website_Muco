## 6. Open findings

**F-01 · S3 · Honest-content ceiling.**
Where a visitor supplies nothing, the preview shows category examples labelled
"Example services — add your own". That is truthful, and it is also visibly less
persuasive than a page full of invented specifics would be. This is a deliberate trade
and the right one, but it means a preview generated from three fields is a weaker
sales artefact than one generated from a filled-in wizard.
*Recommendation:* leave as is; consider nudging the visitor to add services before
generating.

**F-02 · S3 · Script weight.**
`website-preview.js` is roughly 175 KB uncompressed — five complete website templates
with their stylesheets. It is deferred, same-origin, loads on one page only and
compresses well, so no other page pays anything. Still worth stating rather than
discovering later.
*Recommendation:* if a sixth template is ever added, split the templates into a second
file loaded on entering `#/designs`.

**F-03 · S3 · Accessibility verified programmatically, not with assistive technology.**
Semantics, focus management, live regions, `inert` and contrast were all checked by
measurement. No screen reader was actually driven.
*Recommendation:* one pass with NVDA or VoiceOver before this is promoted in
advertising.

**F-04 · S4 · Five type sets from three families.**
`font-src 'self'` permits only the three self-hosted families, so the five type sets
are built from those plus system stacks, differentiated by weight, case, tracking and
scale. They are genuinely distinct, but "Friendly Rounded" from the brief's suggested
list is not among them because no rounded face is available.
*Recommendation:* if this matters commercially, self-host one more woff2.

**F-05 · S4 · No Stats section.**
The brief lists Stats among the section toggles. It is deliberately absent: a stats
band is numbers, we hold no true numbers about the visitor's business, and generating
"500+ happy customers" is the fabrication both the brief and this studio's published
position forbid. Process and Find us are offered instead, because those are things the
visitor actually knows.
*Recommendation:* keep the omission. Revisit only if the wizard starts collecting real
figures.

**F-06 · S4 · Chromium only.**
*Recommendation:* spot-check Safari, since shadow DOM plus container queries plus
`inert` is the combination most likely to differ there.

## 7. Honesty review

This is the part most likely to cause commercial damage if it were wrong, so it was
audited line by line. The feature makes no claim that:

- the preview is AI-generated — it publishes the opposite, as an FAQ answer
- the preview is a publishable website — the page, the FAQ and a sticky stamp on every
  preview all say it is not
- anything was sent to MUCO LABS — the approval screen states plainly that nothing was
- any review, rating, customer count, award, certification, year or price is real —
  none appear, and the testimonial-shaped block is labelled as a layout example with
  unattributed placeholder text
- any photograph shows the visitor's business — placeholders are drawn rather than
  photographic, and captions never claim ownership

No S0–S2 finding in this category.
