## P. SEO audit, 16 September 2026

Measured rather than assumed. Where a claim could not be verified it is marked as such.

### Fixed: two pages arguing for one query

`/services-websites` and `/website-development-erode` shared **30%** of their title, description and `h1` vocabulary with the brand name excluded. Both led on Erode. That splits a local signal rather than doubling it.

The first attempt at the fix was wrong and is worth recording. Removing the city from the service page's *title* alone moved the figure to **33%** — dropping a word shrank the comparison set without touching the cause. The cause was the description, which opened "for businesses in Erode and Tamil Nadu". The service page now sells the service and the Erode page owns the city. Overlap is **18%**, and the remainder is the brand name plus words like "website" that both pages are entitled to.

### The pattern underneath the rest

"Erode", "Tamil" and "Nadu" appear in nearly every page description on the site. Each is individually defensible for a genuinely local business. In aggregate it spreads the local signal thinly across twenty-four pages instead of concentrating it on the two competing for local queries.

**Principle to apply as content changes:** a page should carry the city in its description only if it is trying to rank for that city. `/pricing` does not need it. `/website-development-erode` cannot do without it.

Not applied in bulk this session, deliberately — rewriting twenty descriptions at once to chase a signal should follow evidence, and that evidence is one Search Console connection away.

Remaining pairs above 22%, each judged acceptable: `/` vs `/services` (30%) and `/services-business-systems` vs `/services` (24%) are hub-and-spoke and do not compete; `/` vs `/website-development-erode` (24%) and `/services-mobile` vs `/website-development-erode` (25%) are the geo-dilution pattern above.

### Fixed: title width

Thirteen titles sat under 50 rendered characters, leaving result width unused. Pages competing for a query gained a real modifier rather than a keyword: Android & iOS, Web & Mobile, HRMS, Tamil Nadu. Legal pages were left short because they are not competing for anything.

`site-audit.py` now enforces the 60-character ceiling. Two measurement details are worth keeping: it decodes HTML entities first, because `&amp;` is five characters in source and one on the page and measuring raw text reported titles as over-long that were not; and it skips `/ta`, because a Tamil glyph is far wider than a Latin one and a Latin character count does not describe it.

### Fixed: the local page was under-linked

Counted as contextual links inside `<main>` only — navigation and footer point everywhere from everywhere and would hide a genuine orphan.

`/website-development-erode` is the local money page and had **three** inbound contextual links, **none from the home page**, which is the strongest page on the site. It now has five including home, placed in the FAQ answer that was already a sentence about working across Erode — a descriptive anchor in prose that existed anyway rather than a bolted-on link. `/terms` had zero and now has one, from the payment terms on `/pricing`.

Still thin, in priority order: `/about` (1 inbound, a trust page reached almost only through the nav — worth linking from `/work` and `/pricing`, where a reader is deciding whether to believe you), `/maintenance` (1, belongs at the end of each service page's handover section), `/careers` and `/learning-portal` (1 each, both acceptable).

### Verified clean, and therefore not changed

- Every JSON-LD block on all 32 pages parses. `BreadcrumbList` on all 26 interior pages, `FAQPage` on 14, `Organization` on 31, `Person` carrying the founder's LinkedIn.
- Every `<img>` carries width and height, so layout shift is prevented at source. All 13 are lazy-loaded.
- One stylesheet, **zero render-blocking scripts**, three font preloads.
- `og:title` and `og:description` unique on all 28 English pages. Only `og:image` is shared: generating 32 per-page images needs an imaging dependency, which this generator does not have and Vercel's build would not install. §45 stays deferred for that reason rather than for lack of effort.
- Reciprocal `hreflang` on the four Tamil pairs, `x-default` to English.
- `robots.txt` is deliberate, including why `/404` is not disallowed.

Two findings were raised and then withdrawn after measurement: no title is actually over 60 characters once entities are decoded, and the replacement character visible in `robots.txt` is a real em dash the reviewing terminal could not render. Both are recorded because a withdrawn finding is as useful to the next reader as a confirmed one.
