Website & local search setup

A business website built around customer enquiries.

MUCO LABS helps businesses in Erode and Tamil Nadu put their services online, make contact simple and measure which pages lead to enquiries. We agree the deliverables in a written scope before the build begins.

What the launch scope can include

  1. A clear business website: services, location, real product or work evidence and contact details, designed for phones as well as desktops.
  2. A local search foundation: useful service copy, titles, canonical URLs, sitemap and indexability checks.
  3. Google Business Profile support: review the genuine business name, category, service areas and contact details. Profile access, eligibility and verification remain with the business owner and Google.
  4. An enquiry system: direct WhatsApp and call links plus a public form with confirmed receipt.
  5. Measurement and handover: agreed analytics events, account ownership, source code and editing guidance.

Who this suits

A local service business, shop or professional practice whose customers need to understand the offer before contacting it. If customers need product stock, checkout, delivery or recurring bookings, we scope those workflows separately. A social profile can support discovery; the website provides a stable place for your complete service information.

What we need from you

Your business name, actual service area, contact details, service list, logo and real photos or work examples. We review what is ready and identify content gaps before promising a launch date. You approve the copy and scope before publication.

One-time build and ongoing costs

The quote separates design, development and launch from domain renewal, hosting, email, software subscriptions, maintenance and ongoing SEO. Advertising spend and payment gateway fees are separate. No standard price is published until the scope and commercial terms are agreed.

Read the website cost guide and how we quote.

How we check the result

Before handover we test service links, forms, mobile layouts and crawlable page content. After launch, use actual enquiry records and Search Console data to decide what to improve. Search indexing, ranking and enquiry volume cannot be guaranteed by a website build.

Questions before you start

Is monthly SEO included in the website build?

A launch search foundation can be included in the written scope. Ongoing content, profile updates, monitoring and SEO improvements are a separate recurring service.

Can I speak to you without creating an account?

Yes. Use the public enquiry form, WhatsApp, phone or email. The customer workspace is for agreed project work.

Can you work with a business outside Erode?

Yes. We can scope remote delivery using calls, shared documents and review milestones. Local-profile work must reflect your actual business location and eligibility.
