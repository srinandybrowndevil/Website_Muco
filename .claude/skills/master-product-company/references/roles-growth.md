# Growth, search, and organic-lead roles (all master-level)

These roles sit on every public website and store. They start in R&D, they constrain Design, they produce hooks Frontend must ship, and QA can fail a release that ignores them.

## Roles

- SEO specialist / SEO optimizer — crawl, index, intent, internals, thin/duplicate, Core Web Vitals as ranking hygiene, title/H1/canonical/sitemap/robots
- AEO specialist — Answer Engine Optimization. Question-shaped content, FAQPage/QAPage schema when true, concise extractable answers, entity clarity
- AIO specialist — AI Optimization / AI Overview readiness. Clean facts, consistent NAP/entity, machine-readable specs, no contradictory claims across pages
- GEO specialist — Generative Engine Optimization. Source-worthy pages, citations, original data, quotable definitions, crawlable text not image-only meaning
- SEM specialist — paid search structure that does not fight organic intent map. Query groups, landing-page truth
- SMM specialist — social surfaces, OG/Twitter cards, share states, shoppable posts where relevant
- SSCM / social-search commerce — shopping feeds, product truth in Merchant Center-class feeds, social catalog sync, sponsored + organic product consistency. Use this label when the user says SSCM
- Content strategist / content designer
- CRO specialist — experiment hygiene that never breaks checkout
- Analytics / measurement — event taxonomy, lead definition, consent mode
- Email / lifecycle — only with consent
- Brand search / reputation — SERP/KG hygiene

## Organic lead bar

A public page is not done if a qualified stranger cannot find it, understand it, or trust it enough to act.

R&D writes the intent map. Design places the slots. Frontend implements markup and internals. Backend supplies true data. Tester checks rendered source not just the pretty view. QA fails missing or false growth surfaces on primary templates.

## Do not

- Keyword-stuff
- Invent reviews, ratings, or stock
- Cloak
- Ship three different facts to Google, ChatGPT, and the page body
- Call ads a substitute for indexable usefulness
