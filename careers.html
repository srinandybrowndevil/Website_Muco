Careers & collaboration

We are small, and we hire that way

Most work here is project-based or contract, and we are honest that we are not a large company with a bench. If you are good and you want direct ownership of what you build, there is room.

Straight about the stage we are at. MUCO LABS is founder-led. We do not currently advertise salaried full-time positions, employee counts or benefits packages, because publishing those before they exist would be dishonest. What we do have is real project work, and terms agreed per engagement.

Freelancers & studios

Collaboration, not subcontracting in the dark

We work with independent developers, designers and small studios on projects that need more hands or a specific specialism. If you bring the client, you stay in the relationship — we do not go around you.

Commercial terms, revenue split and ownership are agreed in writing per engagement. We are not publishing a fixed split here until it is confirmed in a form we can hold ourselves to.

How to apply

  • Message us on WhatsApp or email with the role you want
  • Send links to real work — repositories, live sites or apps
  • Tell us what you actually built in each one
  • Mention your availability and how you prefer to be paid

A short honest message about two projects beats a long CV. We read everything ourselves.

Next step

Nothing above matches you?

If you are strong at something we have not listed, tell us what it is and what you have built with it. We would rather hear from you than miss you.
