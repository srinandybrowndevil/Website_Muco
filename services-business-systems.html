Questions

About crm, erp, hrms, lms & billing

Can you move our existing data across?

Usually yes, from spreadsheets, Tally exports or an older system. Migration is scoped and priced as its own task because it is real work and it deserves testing.

Does it handle GST properly?

Invoicing is built to your actual filing requirements, confirmed with whoever handles your accounts. We build to what your accountant needs, not to a generic template.

Can staff use it without training?

That is a design requirement, not an afterthought. If your team needs a manual to record a sale, the interface is wrong.
